ShelfAware is a real-time semantic localization system for quasi-static indoor environments with repetitive geometry, dynamic clutter, and changing object semantics. It fuses visual-inertial odometry, depth likelihoods, and category-centric semantic evidence inside a Monte Carlo localization framework, enabling robust global localization with low-cost sensors.
